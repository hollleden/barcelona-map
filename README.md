# 🗺️ Barcelona — Interactive Districts & Neighbourhoods Map

An interactive map of Barcelona showing all 10 districts and 73 neighbourhoods. Click any district or neighbourhood to learn what its name means, where it came from, and what's worth seeing there.

## What it does

- Shows the full administrative map of Barcelona — 10 districts, 73 neighbourhoods
- Every area has its etymology (where the name comes from) and a short history
- Points of interest for each district with links to Wikipedia and Google Maps
- Works in three languages: Spanish, Catalan, and Russian — switch without reload
- Runs entirely in the browser from a single HTML file, no server needed

## Why

Most maps just show you where things are. This one tells you **why** they're called that. Barcelona's neighbourhood names are full of stories — from Arabic roots to medieval guilds to 19th-century factory owners. The map is a way to explore the city's history through its geography.

Built as a personal research project — not an official city product.

## Who it's for

- Anyone curious about Barcelona's neighbourhoods
- Travellers who want context behind the names on the map
- Locals who want to know what "Poble Sec" or "Vallcarca" actually mean
- Anyone interested in how cities tell their own history

## Data sources

All geometry and history come from public sources: Open Data BCN (official geodata), Ajuntament de Barcelona (historical texts), and Viquipèdia (etymology). All links back to the originals are included in the interface.
